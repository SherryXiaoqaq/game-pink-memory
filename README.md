# game-pink-memory
Hide memories in the game, and then find them together again.  
Every time you clear a level, it's a moment of falling in love again.   
A little game for two people, also a memory for the two of you.
Flip through the album again and look at the wonderful moments belonging just to couples/friends~
Everyone is welcome to play!!
